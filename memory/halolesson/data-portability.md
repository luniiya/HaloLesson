# Future data portability and sharing

*(as of 2026-11-05)*

The user wants these after the current base/UI work:
- Export and import their data.
- Share individual skills.
- Share complete subjects, including their lessons/skills.

Keep these future requirements in mind when settling the generated-lesson format and IDs, but do not move them ahead of the current UI phase.
